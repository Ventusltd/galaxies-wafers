ONE SYSTEM, STOP BY STOP

Five published engine calculations, walked in the order a single-line diagram is drawn: a load, the nearest mapped node, the cable route between them, the physics along that feeder, and the rating labels at a busbar. Each stop adds its element to the diagram. Where one calculation's output becomes the next one's input, the wire is drawn with its unit and meaning, and refused when they do not match.
