Questions
How does this help draw a system or a single-line diagram?
The stated MVA values can annotate illustrative transformer blocks. firmCapacityMva returns the total-minus-largest arithmetic; assessAgainstFirm compares a stated demand with it. No bus topology, actual transformer or real connection is drawn or selected.
What is connected next?
Nothing is established. Transfer paths, upstream constraints, queue position, protection, operating arrangement and security compliance are absent. The numerical remainder is not spare capacity.
What does the unequal-unit case reveal?
For90and30MVA with42MVA demand, losing the90leaves30, below demand. Losing the30would leave90. Being above the largest-loss remainder does not mean that every possible unit outage fails the same arithmetic.
What does one unit mean?
Removing the only stated unit leaves0MVA in this sum. Positive demand divided by that zero gives no finite utilisation percentage. The engine's Infinity is tagged explicitly in JSON, not silently turned into null.
