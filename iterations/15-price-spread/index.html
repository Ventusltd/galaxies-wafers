Questions
How does this help draw a system or a single-line diagram?
It can supply an illustrative transfer-energy or gross-value annotation under stated inputs. No actual link, cable, electrical node or connection is identified. This page draws a numerical sensitivity chart, not route geometry.
Which functions do the arithmetic?
flowDirection, energyTransferredGwh and congestionRentGbp from the pinned interconnector-economics.js. The page owns input bounds, chart coordinates and formatting; every sensitivity value comes from another direct engine call.
What is connected next?
Nothing is established. The output does not supply dispatch, loss modelling, network capacity, a project valuation or profit. A selected price spread cannot establish a real flow schedule.
What happens at equal prices?
The direction function reports no commercial incentive, while the gross-rent function refuses a zero spread. The page shows that exact refusal and does not fabricate a zero-valued engine result.
Machine detail

Pinned source proof · Lab contract

Inputs: positive GW, hours and utilisation in (0, 1]; finite signed prices in GBP/MWh. Interface limits: at most 100 GW, 8,760 hours and absolute prices of 10,000 GBP/MWh. These are interface bounds, not investment assumptions or source-module guarantees. Empty inputs remain missing. Nonfinite outputs are refused.

Outputs: GWh, signed and absolute GBP/MWh spread, commercial-incentive direction and gross GBP. Gross arithmetic converts GWh to MWh inside the module. No actual link inventory is copied. The module's own proof is a verified static caller at this pin; cross-repository callers were not exhaustively audited.
