NO RECORD, NO PERMISSION

A decision record attaches a judgement to one permanent key of the estate: a block, a constant, a function family or a pair of blocks. A decided record is permission to do what it says. An open record is a question: the key is not changed. No record is no permission. Each record is a stone on the board, placed by its number; the keys it judges are the lit points beside it.
