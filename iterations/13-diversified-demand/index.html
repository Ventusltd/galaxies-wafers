Questions
How does this help draw a system or a single-line diagram?
It supplies an illustrative aggregate load label in kW. In assumed mode, diversifiedDemandKw(...).value supplies that label; in inverse mode, the label is the supplied peak. No bus, feeder, transformer or real connection is selected.
Which function is doing the work?
diversifiedDemandKw or impliedCoincidence in engine/diversified-demand.js, at the full source revision below. These are direct imported calls, not a local formula implementation. The module's own proof is a verified static caller at that pin; cross-repository callers have not been exhaustively audited.
Where does the factor come from?
From an explicit assumption, or the ratio implied by a supplied group peak. Neither direction determines whether the inputs represent this group, its control regime or its future behaviour.
What is connected next?
Nothing has been established. A load label alone does not identify a connection point or establish firm capacity, security, voltage, thermal limits or protection suitability.
Machine detail

Pinned calculation source · Lab contract

Inputs: positive integer units, positive kW per unit, and either a fraction in (0, 1] or a positive group peak no greater than the unrestricted total. This lab adds explicit bounds: at most 10,000,000 units, 1,000 kW per unit and a safe integer count. These are interface limits, not engine guarantees or engineering limits.

Output: aggregate group kW, or a dimensionless coincidence factor. This lab computes no annual energy or time-window average, and never presents an average as a peak. Missing, zero, nonfinite and out-of-range values are refused. Invalid input removes the previous result.
